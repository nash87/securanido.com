the warm dwelling
what it is

A private operating system for your whole life.

securanido brings your personal and business life — calendar, contacts, journal, photos, documents — into one coherent system that runs on hardware you own. Open source under Apache-2.0, self-hosted, EU-sovereign, and Rust-native. A fleet of named AI agents does the work, locally by default; you keep the keys, and nothing is encrypted on a server you can’t see.

the architecture

Built like a home you own.

  • Encrypted before it leaves

    Envelope encryption on the device. The server holds an opaque blob — never your plaintext, never your keys. You stay the only party who can read your data.

    keys you hold

  • Your AI runs at home

    A personal prompt with your data never silently reaches a cloud model. Local-by-default routing is a hard rule; the cloud is used only on explicit escalation, with your own key.

    local-first

  • Rust-native, self-hosted

    Every capability is a Rust crate that runs both as a local tool and as a self-hosted service on infrastructure you control. One program, two runtimes — no second implementation to drift, no data leaving your boundary.

    sovereign

“securanido” is a technical claim, not a marketing word.

These are the commitments the architecture is built around — stated plainly, in alpha, with no certification claimed. The data-minimal posture is visible in the public source, not just asserted.

01

End-to-end, before any server contact

Encryption happens on the client. Sync and backup stay operationally blind to your content.

 client-side
02

Hardware-bound keys

The architecture seals your master key to your own device — it never leaves the hardware, and there is no escrow copy held by us, because we are never in the path.

 keys you hold
03

Sensor-access transparency

The direction: every camera, mic, location and contacts access logged to an append-only report you can audit by service and time.

 by design
04

Data portability as a right

One export carries everything — calendar, contacts, journal, photos — in a structured, machine-readable format, importable anywhere. Portability is a right, not a feature we grant.
